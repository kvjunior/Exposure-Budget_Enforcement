# Management problem and present implementation boundary

Current bounded question: choose canonical per-request plans (ADMIT/DEFER/REJECT/ROTATE, optionally
ROTATE then ADMIT) over a fixed observable prefix/request sequence, authentic-assumed authority and
public coalition reach. Minimize the declared action/opportunity cost subject to admission-time
endpoint constraints. All fragment subsets are allowed and usable service is counted separately.
The exact domain and cost arithmetic are in decision_index_contract.md.

This is a foundation for the intended causal intermittent-owner management question. It does not
yet impose real release/deadline/service-level requirements, simulate exogenous outages/production,
or guarantee cumulative disclosure. The 24-versus-25 example shows why cost and served counts must
both be reported and why a timed service objective needs an explicit contract before freezing the
principal evaluation. No proposed controller or superiority claim is established.

Novelty, sound closure, informative baselines, a rigorous formal result and realistic system evidence
remain separate research obligations. If the strengthened model is a restatement of prior work or
makes a trivial policy sufficient, record that and revisit the direction instead of creating a win.
